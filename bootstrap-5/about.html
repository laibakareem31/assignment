ABOUT US

We pay sincere tribute to our respected benefactors, the (late) Mr. Abdul Ghaffar Abdul Rehman (Mithu Patel) and the (late) Mrs. Zubeda Abdul Ghaffar, whose vision and dedication established the Patel Foundation and Hospital. Patel Hospital is committed to patient-centered care, upholding a legacy of compassion, quality, and accessibility. With 260+ beds, 125+ leading consultants, and 60+ specialized departments, it delivers comprehensive healthcare services. Its quality standards are reinforced through accreditations from the College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan, Pakistan Medical & Dental Council, Sindh Healthcare Commission, and recognized certifications, including ISO 9001:2015, RIQAS, CAP, and PCP. Through programs accredited by the Pakistan Nursing Council and Dow University of Health Sciences, the hospital actively advances medical education and future healthcare leadership. Guided by the spirit of giving, our Welfare Programs, supported by generous donations, provide underprivileged patients with complex surgeries and life-saving treatments with dignity and respect. Through a transparent and structured patient welfare system, we strive to remove financial barriers so that quality healthcare remains accessible to those who need it most. Patel Hospitals commitment to social responsibility is reflected in the CSR and community service awards it has earned over the years. These recognitions highlight our ongoing efforts to support vulnerable communities through free medical camps, health awareness initiatives, and accessible healthcare services.
